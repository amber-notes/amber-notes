# Pinto Notes support

Write to **hello@pintonotes.com**. You'll normally get an answer within a day or two.

## Common questions

**How do I sync between my iPhone and my Mac?**
Sign in with the same account on both. Notes sync on their own, within about a second.

**How do I share a note?**
Open the note, choose **Share → Share Link…**, and confirm. The link is copied. Anyone with it can read the note until you choose **Stop Sharing**.

**How do I connect ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code or Codex?**
Open **Settings → Connect an AI** and follow the steps for your assistant. You approve every connection in Pinto Notes, and you can disconnect it there at any time.

**I deleted a note by mistake.**
Deleted notes stay in **Recently Deleted**. Open it, and restore the note.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open **Settings → Account**, scroll to the bottom, and choose **Delete Account…**. This deletes your account and everything in it, and can't be undone. You can also email us to ask for it.

**I want to report a shared page.**
Use the **Report this page** link at the bottom of the page, or write to hello@pintonotes.com with the page's address.
