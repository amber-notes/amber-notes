# Release gate: Pinto Notes 1.2 for Mac, as released

Released 2026-10-10 as build 202610100527 (main 60d3b0af, tag mac-v1.2).

**1.2 is over its budgets on 20 lines and was released anyway.** Emil decided this on 2026-10-09
("we don't need 1.2 to pass the requirements, the app is already way better than before"): for this
release the budgets are targets, and what blocks is a correctness bug, a line a person would feel that
is worse than the released 1.1.2, or a failure in security, storage or network. No budget was changed
or loosened for it; from the next release the full gate applies again. Against 1.1.2, measured the same
evening on the same Mac, nothing a person would feel is worse at 1 or 2,000 notes, and most of what
people feel is better (lists below). The lines over budget are tracked in #348, #349, #350, #353, #354
and #380.

One release blocker was found by the gate's last step, the signed-in update test, and it is not in the
budgets at all: see "The update from 1.1.2" below.

- MacBook Air M4, macOS 27.0.1, staging backend, bench accounts of 1, 2,000 and 20,000 notes, three

- The gate's per-step hang names are off by one step: a hang is filed under the last measured step, and

## Security, storage, network

- Security: 13 of 14 lines pass (secret scan, advisors unchanged, row-level security over 57 tables,
  no note text in the clear, dependency audits, no new hosts, the app called only its backend).
- The fourteenth, "entitlements unchanged since the baseline", fails by design whenever a file changes
  and is passed by hand: the files now take the same values from build settings, add
  `applinks:pintonotes.com` beside ambernotes.app, and the Beta has a file of its own. Reviewed by the
  release lead (Claude), not by Emil; listed for him. For the Mac download there is no change at all:
  the notarized 1.2 app's only entitlement is `com.apple.security.automation.apple-events`, the same
  as the released 1.1.2 (read with `codesign -d --entitlements -` on both). The app group, keychain
  group and applinks lines are in the iPhone and Mac App Store files and are still to be read on the
  first App Store build.
- Storage: 8 lines pass, 5 are larger than 1.1.2 (the sizes above), all inside budget.
- Network: 60 lines pass, 9 are more than 1.1.2 (launch requests and bytes, first-sync bytes), 1 is over
  budget (the staging push above).

## The update from 1.1.2

The test: the released 1.1.2 signed in to a real account on production with three notes and two
pictures, an edit made offline, then the update to a notarized 1.2 candidate from a local feed.

- The update itself works: one `Pinto Notes.app`, one process, Gatekeeper accepts it, the session and
  the local library are kept.
- After it the Mac does not have the account's key and asks for it. Cause, proven from the system log:
  the 1.1.2 Mac download never saved the key. The system refused every write to the protected keychain
  (-34018, the build has no keychain group) while reads were not refused, so 1.1.2 believed the key was
  stored and held it in memory only. Reopening 1.1.2 itself shows the same (tried). 1.2 tests a write,
  uses its fallback store and keeps the key from then on, but nothing can rescue a key the old app only
  held in memory, and an update is a relaunch.
- For a person with another signed-in device or a saved recovery key this is one prompt. For a person
  with only the Mac, the way forward is "No device left?", "None of these work", Start fresh. Tried on
  a staging account with its saved key removed: the notes, folders and a picture on the Mac stay and
  are uploaded again under a new key within seconds; version history, shared links and AI connections
  are gone. The screen used to say "your account starts empty"; #393 changed the text to say the notes
  on this Mac are kept, and the update's release notes say what will happen.
- Not tried: a Mac whose local library is incomplete, and Start fresh pressed on another device.
- With a saved key, a 1.2 build opens and edits notes with no network and syncs the edit after (tried).
- The released build was then installed from the live feed over the candidate: offered and installed
  on the first try, Gatekeeper accepts it.
- The Dock keeps pointing at `Amber Notes.app`, which is gone after the update (the changelog says so),
  and the login item keeps its old name.
