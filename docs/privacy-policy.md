# Amber Notes privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Amber Notes is a notes app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Emil Wagman. This page says what it stores, where, and what it never does.

## What Amber Notes stores

- **Your account.** When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives Amber Notes an identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your email address and name. If you choose Hide My Email, we only ever see Apple's relay address.
- **Your notes, folders and files.** Everything you write, and the images and files you add, so they can sync between your devices.
- **Earlier versions of your notes,** so a change can be undone.
- **Connections you make.** If you connect an AI assistant (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code or Codex), we store which connections exist and when each was last used, so you can see and revoke them.
- **Share links you create.** If you share a note, we store the link so the page can show it, until you stop sharing.

## Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). It travels encrypted (HTTPS), and each account can only ever read its own notes: the database enforces this on every request.

## What Amber Notes never does

- No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data.
- No one reads your notes. The only way anything leaves your account is what you choose:
  - **AI connections:** an assistant you connect can read (and, if you allow it, change) your notes on your behalf. What that assistant does with them is covered by its own privacy policy. You can disconnect it at any time in Settings.
  - **Share links:** a note you share can be read by anyone who has its link, until you stop sharing it. Shared pages are hidden from search engines.

## Deleting your data

- Delete a note, and it moves to Recently Deleted; delete it there and it's gone, including its earlier versions.
- **Settings → Delete Account** deletes your account and everything in it: notes, files, versions, connections and share links. This can't be undone.
- You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to emil@norditech.se.
