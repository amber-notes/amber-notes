# Code of conduct

Amber Notes follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't accepted anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or email.

To report a problem, email **hello@ambernotes.app**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept private.
